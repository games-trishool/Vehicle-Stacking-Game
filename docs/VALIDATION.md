# Validation — 1.4.0

The prepared cloud environment builds and runs the game. All production assets, including music, are bundled locally.

## Expanded fleet — 1.4.0

- `npm test`: **41 tests passed**, with no failures or skips. Fourteen new checks cover full catalog/spec consistency, independent gameplay bodies, three rickshaw wheels and three-axle heavy trucks, centered Cannon ES drops for all six additions, first platform/ground contacts after misses, individual unlock costs, persistence, and requiring all 12 vehicles for newly earned collector achievements. The prior progression, audio, physics, and ad tests also pass.
- `npm run build` and `npx cap sync android` passed. All 12 vehicle types generate their picker thumbnails, using model bounds to fit larger trucks and the rickshaw without clipping. The picker count is derived from the catalog, now 12 actual vehicles plus Mixed mode. The original six collision shapes and masses are preserved through the shared body factory.
- The actual 3D models were rendered into [the new-vehicle gallery](fleet-additions-1.4.0.png) and [the full fleet gallery](fleet-1.4.0.png), and visually reviewed. All mesh positions/normals were checked for finite values. The [model report](vehicle-metrics-1.4.0.json) records 10–14 material batches and 11,444–25,430 triangles for the six additions. Templates, materials, and geometry remain shared across drops. Emergency lenses add no point lights. Model geometry and software-renderer checks do not establish Pixel 8 frame rates.
- All **four focused browser checks passed** (5.7 minutes). The fleet check buys all six additions through the real picker, verifies thumbnail images, balances, selected-only gameplay, persisted purchases after reload, and the 9/12 unlock count. Normal centered inputs earn actual landing rewards with both the rickshaw and mixer. The mobile check reaches all 13 cards by real touch swipes across small portrait/landscape layouts while Cancel/Done remain fixed. Existing real-physics/replay and native-bridge third-game ad checks also pass.
- Android `bundleRelease` passed. Google bundletool 1.17.1 validation passed for `Stack-Vechiles-1.4.0-unsigned.aab` (**8,224,668 bytes**). Its package is `com.trishool.stackline`, version code **7**, version **1.4.0**, minimum API 23, target API 35, with no debuggable flag. ZIP integrity and byte equality of all **31 production web assets** passed; SHA-256 is in `releases/SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- The local signing helper passed both new-key and existing-key flows using a temporary key. Independent `jarsigner` verification passed; temporary keys and signed outputs were deleted. No production upload key is included. Sign the shared unsigned bundle with the owner's existing key.
- Physical Pixel 8 checks remain necessary for tall mixed towers, new-truck handling, startup time, sound balance, warmth, and native ad/activity behavior. Browser play still has no native AdMob ads; Android uses Google test IDs.

## Previous regression baseline — 1.2.0

The broader regression results below were recorded for 1.2.0. Later focused checks are reported above; the full browser suite and physical-device checks were not rerun for 1.3.0.

- `npm test`: all **20 tests passed**. Eleven cover rewards, purchases, persistence, legacy saves, and reset; four use real Cannon ES simulation for safe stacking and first platform/ground contact; three check impact filtering and finite, peak-bounded crash samples at 32/48 kHz. Two audio-controller regressions use a suspended-device boundary: the first crash is scheduled even before the audio state changes to running, and prepared music starts its source synchronously in the Play call without fetching or rendering.
- `npm run build`: strict TypeScript compilation and Vite production bundling passed. Normal builds use the checked-in **240,812-byte** original Ogg score. The optional score-authoring script was also executed successfully with Chromium and ffmpeg. Track duration is 15.238094 seconds. No score composition runs in the shipped Play handler.
- All **12 browser checks passed across the regression run and targeted rerun**, using Chromium and software WebGL. Eleven passed in the full run; the touch check caught a 5px overflow in the updated help explanation at 320×480. That copy was shortened, and the complete touch-layout check passed against the final build. No tests or assertions were disabled or relaxed.
- The music browser check confirms that the track is decoded before Play, the playback source starts within 50ms of the controller's Play request, and the actual Web Audio analyser has audible output. Pause/resume, independent effects/music settings, saved music-off, and re-enabling music are exercised. This checks scheduling and the audio graph, not phone-speaker latency.
- A fresh-launch crash check uses no prior menu interaction, starts music on the first Play gesture, and drops a vehicle through normal input. It requires exactly one crash playback with nonzero effects output on the first collision, before any landing reward. It then disables Sound effects, reloads, repeats the collision, and verifies silence while music remains enabled.
- Selection/purchase tests verify that selecting a card alone does not change the saved choice. × discards it and spends no coins. Done commits a free selection or charges the selected unlock once. Vehicle and place purchases survive reload. Insufficient coins leave the purchase uncommitted. Forest, Night City, and Space are free; the remaining places cost 150/250/350 coins, matching the three paid vehicle tiers.
- Legacy saves retain balances and previously purchased vehicles. Without explicit place ownership, only the three free places are unlocked; an old selected paid place falls back to Forest. New or reset saves start with zero coins. Combo multipliers change points only: normal landings give one coin and perfects give three at every combo size.
- The real-physics browser check performs three centered drops using normal keyboard input, verifies nine earned coins, score/perfect bonuses, pause/resume, then a miss and clean replay. No physics bodies, positions, scores, or clocks are edited by tests. A corner miss still ends on its first platform contact; result buttons remain separated. Restart/home remove previous falling vehicles.
- Reset is exercised through the real confirmation alert. Cancelling preserves the save. Confirming clears balances, purchased vehicles/places, scores, heights, runs, achievements, totals, choices, and settings; it returns home with free choices and zero run counters. The fresh save survives reload. A separate unit check verifies that unrelated browser storage is untouched.
- Touch layouts cover 320×480, 320×568, 360×640, 390×844, 412×915, 844×390, and 1366×768. The page stays fixed. Real touch gestures reach every item in the selection lists while headers and Done remain outside scrolling content. Settings with Reset game, help, play, pause, achievements, and local rankings fit or use their intended list/page controls.
- Rendering/lifecycle checks verify idle menus/pauses stop scheduling and rendering, settings request a redraw, and resuming restarts the scene. Auto/High/Battery saver select the expected pixel ratio, shadow, and city-light budgets at a touch viewport with device scale 2.625. Simulated visibility events pause a live run and require player input to resume. Native lifecycle and physical Pixel 8 performance still need device testing.
- The detailed vehicle geometry is unchanged from 1.1.0. Its six-model gallery, counts, and historical rendering measurements remain available in [PERFORMANCE.md](PERFORMANCE.md). No new physical-device FPS or thermal claim is made by this UI/audio/progression update.

These results verify builds and browser functionality. Physical Pixel 8 sound balance, output latency, frame times, thermals, vibration, Android WebView/lifecycle behavior, and current Google Play requirements remain device/store checks. Rankings remain local. Follow the [device checklist](PERFORMANCE.md#pixel-8-checks-before-the-next-release) before rollout.
