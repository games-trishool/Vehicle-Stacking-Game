Privacy policy

Your game progress

Stack Vehicles saves scores, recent runs, settings, coins, diamonds, unlocks and achievements on your device so you can continue playing. You can play without creating a game account.

Advertising

The Android game uses Google AdMob for banners, interstitials and optional rewarded ads. This version requests non-personalized test ads. Google’s SDK can still send IP addresses, device identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics to Google for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. An IP address can indicate approximate location. See Google’s SDK disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Google Play handles Android checkout and payment details. The game uses product identifiers and transaction information to check purchases, deliver items and prevent duplicate rewards. It does not ask for your card details.

Retention and deletion

Reset game clears gameplay progress. Purchase-delivery records can survive a reset to prevent duplicate grants. To remove local game data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings or clear this site’s data in your browser. This can permanently erase your progress. Clearing local data does not delete Google’s purchase history or advertising records. Google manages those records under its own policies.

Privacy questions

For questions about your game data, contact the developer using the support contact on the store listing where you obtained the game. For data held by Google, visit Google Account privacy controls.
